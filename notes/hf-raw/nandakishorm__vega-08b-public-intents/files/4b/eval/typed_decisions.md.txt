Paired (same 2050 decisions, McNemar): after vs before fine-tuning 703 / 94 (p=4.6e-116).
Two calibrations were fitted on the held-out train cases: against the correct answers (default; confidence tracks accuracy) and against the teacher's distributions (probabilities track the teacher's spread, lower KL, underconfident top label). Load the second with Vega(..., calibration="teacher").
Fine-tuning: best epoch 3, calibration-split soft NLL 1.5589 -> 0.5666, calibration mode thermal. Brier is summed over options against the gold distribution; the card may define it differently, so compare accuracy and soft accuracy with the card rows.

## Adapter (PEFT without forgetting)

The base engine is frozen and saved unchanged. One low-rank adapter (1431905 trainable params, 4.5% of the engine) learned typed-decisions; a gate per question decides whether it applies (threshold 0.50). A closed gate returns the base engine's output exactly.
